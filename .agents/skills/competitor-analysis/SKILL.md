# Competitor Analysis

## When to Apply
Use this skill when analyzing competitors, conducting SWOT analyses, comparing features and pricing, or positioning your product in the market.

## Core Concepts
- SWOT Analysis: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats
- Feature Comparison: Product capabilities and gaps
- Pricing Analysis: Value-based and competitive pricing
- Market Positioning: Perceptual maps and positioning statements
- Competitive Advantage: Sustainable differentiators
- Porter's Five Forces: Industry competition analysis
- Value Chain Analysis: Internal activities and cost structure
- Benchmarking: Performance comparison metrics

## Best Practices
- Identify both direct and indirect competitors
- Update analysis quarterly or when market changes
- Use objective data, not just perceptions
- Look for unmet needs competitors are ignoring
- Analyze competitor responses to market changes
- Understand competitor business models and unit economics
- Monitor competitor hiring and investment patterns
- Use positioning maps to visualize market landscape
- Document assumptions and data sources
- Share findings across teams for alignment

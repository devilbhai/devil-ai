# Market Research

## When to Apply
Use this skill when conducting market research, analyzing industry trends, sizing markets, or understanding customer needs and competitive landscapes.

## Core Concepts
- Market Sizing: TAM, SAM, SOM calculations
- Competitor Analysis: Direct, indirect, potential competitors
- Customer Research: Personas, pain points, buying behavior
- Trend Analysis: Technology adoption, market shifts
- Industry Reports: Market data, growth projections
- Competitive Intelligence: Strengths, weaknesses, strategies
- Value Proposition: Unique selling points, differentiation
- Go-to-Market: Channel strategy, pricing models

## Implementation
```markdown
# Market Research Framework

## 1. Market Definition
- Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- Serviceable Available Market (SAM)
- Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)

## 2. Industry Analysis
- Market size and growth rate
- Key trends and drivers
- Regulatory environment
- Technology landscape

## 3. Competitor Analysis
| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses | Market Share |
|------------|-----------|------------|--------------|
| Comp A     | Brand, scale | Slow innovation | 35% |
| Comp B     | Technology | Limited reach | 20% |

## 4. Customer Segmentation
- Demographics
- Psychographics
- Behavior patterns
- Needs and pain points

## 5. Competitive Positioning
- Value proposition canvas
- Positioning map
- Differentiation strategy
```

## Best Practices
- Use multiple data sources for validation
- Quantify market size with bottom-up and top-down approaches
- Interview potential customers for primary research
- Monitor competitor changes and announcements
- Track technology trends affecting the market
- Validate assumptions with data
- Update research regularly as markets evolve
- Document sources and methodology
- Consider both current and future market states
- Use frameworks (Porter's Five Forces, PESTEL) for structured analysis

# Social Engineering

## When to Apply
Use this skill for authorized social engineering assessments, phishing campaign testing, security awareness training, and human factor vulnerability testing.

## Core Concepts
- Phishing and spear-phishing
- Pretexting and impersonation
- Baiting and tailgating
- Vishing (voice phishing)
- Smishing (SMS phishing)
- Physical social engineering

## Phishing Campaigns
- Email phishing
- Spear-phishing (targeted)
- Whaling (executive targeting)
- Clone phishing
- Business Email Compromise (BEC)
- Phishing-as-a-Service platforms

## Pretexting Scenarios
- IT support impersonation
- Vendor/contractor impersonation
- Executive impersonation
- Job applicant pretext
- Delivery/service pretext
- Emergency/urgency scenarios

## Physical Social Engineering
- Tailgating/piggybacking
- Baiting (USB drops)
- Impersonation
- Dumpster diving
- Shoulder surfing
- Physical access testing

## Awareness Training
- Phishing recognition
- Reporting procedures
- Verification techniques
- Password hygiene
- Physical security awareness
- Incident response

## Legal Requirements
- Written authorization required
- Define scope clearly
- No actual harm to individuals
- Confidentiality of findings
- Follow company policies
- Document all activities

## Tools
- **Gophish** - Phishing simulation
- **SET** - Social Engineering Toolkit
- **Evilginx2** - Advanced phishing
- **King Phisher** - Campaign management
- **BeEF** - Browser exploitation
- **Pretexting scripts** - Custom scenarios

## Campaign Metrics
- Click rate
- Report rate
- Credential submission rate
- Time to report
- Department comparison
- Improvement over time

## Best Practices
- Always get authorization
- Focus on education, not punishment
- Provide immediate feedback
- Track improvement over time
- Make training engaging
- Simulate real-world scenarios
- Protect sensitive findings

# Tender Writer

## When to Apply
Use this skill when preparing tender submissions for government contracts or corporate procurement. Apply when you need to create compliant, competitive tender responses with technical and financial components.

## Core Concepts
- Tender Documentation: Understanding tender requirements and submission format
- Compliance Checklist: Ensuring all mandatory requirements are addressed
- Technical Specifications: Detailed technical approach and methodology
- Financial Bids: Pricing structure, cost breakdowns, and pricing strategy
- Evaluation Criteria: Understanding scoring mechanisms and weightings
- Risk Management: Identifying and mitigating project risks
- Quality Assurance: Quality management systems and certifications
- Past Performance: Demonstrating relevant experience and track record

## Best Practices
- Follow the exact submission format specified in tender documents
- Address every evaluation criterion explicitly
- Use the client's language and terminology
- Provide concrete examples and evidence
- Be precise with pricing and avoid assumptions
- Submit before the deadline with buffer time
- Include all required certifications and forms
- Cross-reference technical and financial proposals
- Highlight unique differentiators and competitive advantages
- Review compliance matrix multiple times

## 🔊 Sound Effects

### **JARVIS-style Sounds:**
```
1. Boot Sequence
   - Musical notes (C4, E4, G4, C5)
   - Smooth fade-in/out

2. Click Sound
   - Short, crisp
   - 800Hz frequency

3. Success Sound
   - Happy chord
   - 523.25Hz (C5)

4. Error Sound
   - Low buzz
   - 200Hz square wave

5. Notification Sound
   - Double beep
   - 880Hz (A5)

6. Thinking Sound
   - Soft hum
   - 440Hz triangle wave

7. Complete Sound
   - Completion chord
   - 659.25Hz (E5)
```
